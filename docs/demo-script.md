# Demo video script (about 2 minutes 30 seconds)

Record in a wide browser window with the "test sats" banner visible. Turn demo mode on so payments go out every 30 seconds. If the default mint charges fees, run with `NUTCAST_MINTS=https://nofees.testnut.cashu.space` so every small payment is visible.

**0:00 Title.** "Nutcast: stream sats in ecash while you listen, with a budget and a pause. Test mint only, no real money."

**0:10 Fund.** Click "Get invoice". Show the invoice, then the balance. "The listener funds once from any Lightning wallet. Today the test mint pays the invoice itself."

**0:25 The split.** Point at "Who gets paid": host 80, guest 15, hosting fee 5. "This is the show's Podcasting 2.0 value block. Every recipient is paid in ecash."

**0:35 Stream.** Set 4 sats per minute and an episode budget of 20. Click "Start streaming sats", press play. Point at "set aside" growing. "Only what I hear counts." Drag the seek bar forward: "Seeking does not."

**1:05 First payment.** After 30 seconds of listening a stream receipt appears with three recipients. "A few sats, split three ways. Many Lightning addresses will not take payments this small. Ecash can."

**1:20 Pause.** Click "Pause streaming" while the audio keeps playing. "Set aside stops growing. I am still listening, I am just not paying." Start it again.

**1:35 Budget.** Raise the rate so the cap is reached. Show "budget reached" and "left in budget: 0". "Mint fees come out of the budget, never on top. It is a hard limit."

**1:50 Boost.** Type "Great episode, thank you!", send 64 sats. Show the receipt.

**2:05 Creator.** Open `/creator`. Show the boost message and the stream payments, listed separately. "These tokens are locked to the creator's key. The server cannot spend them." Click "Redeem all".

**2:20 Cash out.** "Get a test invoice", "Show fees", "Send to Lightning". "Creators cash out to the Lightning wallet they already use."

**2:30 Close.** "Open source, MIT, built in November for Nut November. Thanks to Nodesignal and the Cashu community."

If any segment fails on the day, cut it. A clean stream, pause and claim is the story.
